# Six-color brand trial

Classification: DEVELOPMENT appearance and packaging review.

[Overview](six-color-overview.png) · [Board vector](six-color-07-back.svg) · [Print card](output/pdf/six-color-retail-purchase-card.pdf) · [Bleed card](output/pdf/six-color-retail-purchase-card-bleed.pdf).

Restricted to white, black, PulseSensor red #FF5A5F, WebSerial LED green #44FF44, gray #C0C0C0 and beige #E7DDC8. Green remains LED; red remains power. Board amplifier area is black with white lettering; lighter areas have black lettering. Card amplifier area uses gray, with black reserved for its schematic and text for readability. Beige supplies the warm packaging tone.

Layouts, physical components and circuit connections remain unchanged. Board label polygons are split at the existing dark-region boundary only to change their fill; no footprint or electrical edits. All 40 terminals in the independent map match the native source and both PDF sizes are checked. Prior versions retained; sheet 30, artifact 67.

This is an appearance study. Manufacturer considerations remain in the prior quiet-engineering reality check; no fabrication files were submitted or ordered.
